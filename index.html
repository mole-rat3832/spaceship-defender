HOW TO PLAY

THE MISSION

Guard the mothership. Meteors don't destroy it - they knock it off course. Drift past the red boundary and the mission fails.

After a hit it is destabilized, then its autopilot steers it back.

YOUR DEFENDER

Your ship can take 3 hits (more with hull upgrades). The last one ends the game.

Classic: rotate + thrust. Direct: move any way. Mouse aim: move with keys, aim with the mouse.

BLASTER

SPACE or LEFT-CLICK fires. Every shot adds heat - overheat and you must wait to cool.

Hot blasters can jam. Tap F to clear it!

METEORS

Rock: 1 hit, splits apart. Magma: red-hot, takes 2 hits. Iron: several hits, pushes hard. Comets: tiny and fast.

SHOP & QUESTS

Destroying meteors earns credits. Spend them in the hangar shop on upgrades (shields, piercing bolts, salvage drones and more) and paint jobs.

Finish daily quests for bonus credits.

40 LEVELS, 40 TWISTS

Every level has a twist: solar wind, darkness, EMP blasts, minefields, cloaked meteors, crossed controls and more.

Infinite Voyage never ends - each new sector rolls a random twist.
